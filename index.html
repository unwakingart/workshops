[ The workshop ]

This is a workshop for complete beginners. Everything is set up for you, and the drawing is already on your canvas.

What you'll learn

  • To see a face as five simple shapes of light and dark.
  • To mix every colour in the portrait from just four paints: yellow ochre, vermilion, ivory black and titanium white.
  • To paint from dark to light, the way oil painters do.

What's included

  • A canvas with the drawing already on it.
  • Oil paints, brushes, a palette and medium.
  • A printed guide with every mixing recipe.
  • Your finished portrait to take home.
  • A free written guide on how to improve your painting, after class.

Not included

  • One food item and one drink at the café, which you order and pay for at the café.

[ How to register ]

Registration process

1

Choose your day

The days and cafés are under Workshop fee. It's the same workshop each day, in a small group.

2

Pay the workshop fee

Scan the QR code in the registration form with your banking app or e-wallet. The form shows the fee for the day you're paying: early bird until a week before your workshop, regular after that, and walk-in on the day itself.

3

Submit the form and send the screenshot

Fill in your name, Instagram handle and payment reference number, then submit. Then message @unwakingart on Instagram with a screenshot of your payment receipt. Your registration isn't complete until I get the screenshot.

In your message, include your workshop day and the name or Instagram handle you registered with. After you submit, the form gives you this message ready to copy.

Example: Hi! I registered for the Beginner Oil Painting Portrait workshop.
Day: Sat, Nov 28
Name: Ana Reyes (@anareyes)
Here's my payment receipt.

4

Get your confirmation

I check every payment myself and message you on Instagram to confirm your seat. Your seat is yours once your payment is confirmed.

5

See you at the café

We start at 11:30am. Order your food and drink at the café when you arrive.

Refunds and transfers

  • The workshop fee is non-refundable.
  • Your seat is transferable. If you can't come, you may give your seat to someone else. Message @unwakingart on Instagram with their name before the workshop.

Photos and videos

  • I take photos and videos during the workshop, and unwakingart uses them for marketing and advertising.

Materials and allergies

  • We paint with oil paints, painting mediums and solvents. These can cause reactions in people with allergies, asthma or other sensitivities.
  • You are responsible for knowing your own allergies and sensitivities. If you have any, please check with your doctor before you register.
  • You join the workshop at your own risk. unwakingart and the café are not liable for allergic or other reactions to the materials.
